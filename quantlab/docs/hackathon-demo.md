# QuantLab Hackathon & Presentation Demo Script

Welcome to the QuantLab demonstration. This guide provides a rapid 5-minute walkthrough for judges, clients, and technical teams.

---

## 5-Minute Tour Checklist

### 1. Landing Page & Value Proposition (`/`)
- Showcase the quantitative design aesthetics: deep space dark theme, glassmorphic cards, live market telemetry ticker.
- Review the core modular architecture and interactive value pillars.

### 2. Multi-Asset Terminal (`/market-analysis`)
- Select **NVIDIA (NVDA)**, **Bitcoin (BTC)**, and **Gold (GC=F)**.
- Toggle between Line, Candlestick, and Sub-Indicator charts (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands).
- Point out real-time technical stats: 30-day Volatility, 1-Year Sharpe Ratio, and Current Market Regime.

### 3. Cross-Asset Correlation Lab (`/correlation-lab`)
- Inspect the NxN correlation heatmap matrix.
- View the 60-day rolling correlation chart comparing Bitcoin vs Gold (demonstrating safe-haven flight vs digital store-of-value decoupling).
- Check the pair spread and statistical cointegration score.

### 4. Algorithmic Strategy Builder (`/strategy-builder`)
- Select the **EMA Trend Ribbon** or **Mean Reversion** strategy template.
- Adjust parameters (Fast EMA, Slow EMA, RSI Thresholds, Stop Loss %, Take Profit %).
- Test syntax rules with live validation.

### 5. High-Speed Backtesting Engine (`/backtesting`)
- Run a backtest of the **Dual SMA Crossover** on **Bitcoin (BTC)** from 2022 to 2024.
- Observe:
  - Cumulative Returns vs Benchmark
  - Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, and Max Drawdown KPIs
  - Underwater Drawdown area chart
  - Individual Trade execution log with win/loss PnL attribution.

### 6. Robustness & Monte Carlo Lab (`/robustness-lab`)
- Execute a 1,000-iteration Monte Carlo bootstrap simulation.
- Show the 5th, 50th, and 95th percentile terminal equity paths.
- View the Walk-Forward optimization matrix to verify that the strategy isn't overfitted to past noise.

### 7. Market Regime Radar (`/market-regimes`)
- Inspect the 4-state Hidden Markov / Volatility regime classifier.
- Show historical regime overlays directly over the price series.

### 8. Institutional Research Tear Sheet (`/research-report`)
- Generate an institutional research report.
- Export as PDF or print-ready formatted tear sheet.
